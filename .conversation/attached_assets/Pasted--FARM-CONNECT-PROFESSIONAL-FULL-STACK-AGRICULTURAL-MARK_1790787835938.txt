# FARM CONNECT — PROFESSIONAL FULL-STACK AGRICULTURAL MARKETPLACE

Design and develop a **complete, production-style full-stack web application called “Farm Connect”**.

Farm Connect is a modern online agricultural marketplace that directly connects **Farmers** with **Business Persons/Buyers**. Farmers can publish their agricultural products, farm information, images, availability, expected prices, and other details. Business persons can discover farmers and products, contact farmers, negotiate prices, place orders, and complete online payments.

The application must be designed as a **Database Management Systems (DBMS) project**, so the database, relationships, CRUD operations, authentication, authorization, transactions, validations, APIs, and reporting must actually work.

Do NOT create a static website or UI-only prototype. Every important button, form, navigation item, dashboard card, filter, CRUD operation, search function, contact function, order operation, and payment workflow must be functional.
